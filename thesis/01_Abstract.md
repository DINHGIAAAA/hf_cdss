# ABSTRACT

**Title:** Development of a Clinical Decision Support System for Heart Failure Using Knowledge Graph and GraphRAG

**Field:** Information Technology  
**Code:** 9480104  
**Keywords:** Clinical Decision Support System, Heart Failure, Knowledge Graph, GraphRAG, Large Language Model

---

Heart failure remains a major global health burden, yet only a small fraction of patients with reduced ejection fraction receive full guideline-directed medical therapy. This thesis develops a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) that combines a medical Knowledge Graph with GraphRAG and a large language model to deliver personalized, evidence-based treatment recommendations. An automated pipeline extracts clinical knowledge from FDA drug labels and practice guidelines into structured rules, vector embeddings, and graph relations. The system analyzes patient profiles via chat, recommends GDMT adjustments, checks interactions and dosing safety, and explains outputs in Vietnamese and English. Evaluation on curated clinical cases achieved 94.0% recommendation accuracy, 8.1 seconds average response time, and 4.22/5.0 user satisfaction. The results indicate that Knowledge Graph-augmented retrieval can support safer, faster heart-failure decision making while keeping clinicians in the loop.
